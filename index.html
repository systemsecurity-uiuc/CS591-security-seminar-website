About the seminar

Research across the foundations and systems of security and privacy.

Currently coordinated by Luyi Xing and Ning Luo (Fall 2026), the seminar brings together students, faculty, and invited speakers. Talks span foundational and theoretical research—including cryptography—as well as systems research, emerging challenges, and new approaches to trustworthy computing.

Everyone in the Illinois community is welcome to attend.
